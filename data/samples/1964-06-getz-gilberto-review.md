Recorded in March 1963 and issued by Verve this spring, *Getz/Gilberto* pairs the tenor saxophonist with guitarist-singer João Gilberto and composer Antônio Carlos Jobim at the piano. The surprise of the session is Astrud Gilberto, João's wife, who sings the English verses of "The Girl from Ipanema" in a cool, untrained voice that has already carried the single onto the pop charts.

Jobim's tunes — "Desafinado", "Corcovado", "Só Danço Samba" — give Getz long, lyrical lines to float over the whispered guitar pulse. Milton Banana's drumming is barely audible, which is the point.

Four and a half stars. Bossa nova's defining record.
