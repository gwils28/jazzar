The premise of *Time Out* — an album of originals in unusual meters — could have been a gimmick. That it is not owes much to Paul Desmond, whose "Take Five" in 5/4 rides a two-chord piano vamp with a lightness the odd meter never disturbs. Joe Morello's drum solo on the same track is a model of restraint.

"Blue Rondo à la Turk" alternates a 9/8 theme (grouped 2+2+2+3) with conventional 4/4 blues choruses; Brubeck's block chords are heavier here than Desmond's floating alto would seem to want. Eugene Wright's bass keeps the experiments honest.

Four stars. The quartet has made a popular record without pandering, and "Take Five" will be heard for a long time.
