Recorded in a single evening, December 9, 1964, at Van Gelder Studio in Englewood Cliffs, *A Love Supreme* is a four-part suite — "Acknowledgement", "Resolution", "Pursuance", "Psalm" — performed by the classic quartet: Coltrane (tenor saxophone), McCoy Tyner (piano), Jimmy Garrison (bass) and Elvin Jones (drums).

"Acknowledgement" builds from Garrison's four-note bass motif into a chant of the title phrase, the only vocal passage Coltrane has committed to record. "Pursuance" is the suite's hard-driving centerpiece, Jones's polyrhythms pushing the leader into long, modal flights. "Psalm" is a wordless recitation of the poem printed on the sleeve, each phrase of the saxophone tracking a line of the text.

Five stars. The album is at once Coltrane's most devotional statement and his most disciplined.
