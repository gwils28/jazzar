*Illustrative poll results written for the Jazzar sample corpus; they are not the historical DownBeat poll.*

The 1965 sample critics poll confirms the ascendancy of the Coltrane quartet: the saxophonist takes the tenor category for the fourth consecutive year and *A Love Supreme* is voted record of the year by a wide margin. Elvin Jones, his drummer, wins his category for the first time.

Miles Davis retains the trumpet chair ahead of Dizzy Gillespie. In the alto saxophone category Ornette Coleman edges out Cannonball Adderley, a sign that the critics have made their peace with the "new thing". Thelonious Monk leads the pianists, with Bill Evans second. Duke Ellington's orchestra remains the critics' big band of choice, as it has been for most of the poll's history.
